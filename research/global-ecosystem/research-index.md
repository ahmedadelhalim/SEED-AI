## Executive Overview

This index catalogues the comprehensive research deliverables produced to guide the completion of SEED-AI into a production-grade, offline-first personal AI operating assistant for Windows. SEED-AI remains strictly anchored around its core architectural invariant: **SEED is the sole central authority for request interpretation, model selection, tool execution, permission gating, human approvals, memory ownership, task lifecycle management, and audit logging.** External frameworks, models, voice engines, and tools operate purely as subordinate adapters.
