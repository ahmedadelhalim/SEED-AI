## 1. Governance & Trust Policies (User-Approved)
- **Third-Party Skill Trust (MANUAL):** Administrative review required; provenance inspection and cryptographic digest (`sha256`) binding enforced. Skills cannot grant themselves permissions.
- **MCP Server Trust (EXPLICIT):** Discovered servers remain inactive until explicitly configured in `mcp_servers.json` and approved.
